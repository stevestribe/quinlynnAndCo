The Artisans

A quiet practice, measured in well-made things.

Quinlynn & Co. is the work of a single pair of hands — Ashley's. A homemaker, a mother of six, and a maker who has spent years turning still evenings into objects made with care. Bags lined in linen. Pouches finished by hand. Pieces meant to be used, and used for a long time.

”I'd rather finish one thing well than ten things quickly. Everything in this studio leaves only when it would meet my own table.”

Each piece is cut, sewn, and pressed slowly — often over several mornings. Materials are chosen for how they age: linen that softens, cotton that drapes, thread that disappears into the seam. Custom work is welcomed, and treated with the same patient attention.

— Ashley
